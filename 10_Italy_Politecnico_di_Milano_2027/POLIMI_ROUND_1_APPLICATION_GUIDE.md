## 💰 DSU Lombardia Scholarship Value (100% Full Ride)
All international students admitted to Politecnico di Milano are eligible to apply for the regional **DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) Lombardia** scholarship based on family income:
* **Tuition:** **100% Waived** (Zero tuition fees).
* **Accommodation:** **Free Student Dormitory / University Residence** in Milan.
* **Cash Grant:** Up to **€7,650 / year in cash** deposited to applicant's Italian bank account.
* **Meals:** **1 Free Meal per day** at campus university dining halls.
* **Requirements:** Certified Ethiopian family income and property affidavits converted into an **ISEE Parificato** by an Italian CAF (submitted in July/August after university admission).
