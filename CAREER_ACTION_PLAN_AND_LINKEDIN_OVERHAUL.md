## 4. Interview Preparation: The "Career Gap" Script

When an interviewer asks:  
**"You graduated in 2022. What have you been working on over the last two years?"**

> **Your Answer:**  
> *"Right after graduating with my BSc in Computer Science, I worked at Aduutech developing web systems and inventory platforms. Over the past couple of years, to maintain financial independence and support myself, I worked in transport operations while dedicating all my remaining time to building and shipping production software.*  
>  
> *Rather than just following tutorials, I built real-world products end to end: I built Addis Condo Finder—a crowdsourced geospatial mobile app in Flutter backed by Laravel and OpenStreetMap; I engineered LuckyDraw, a Telegram Mini App with atomic row-locking and integer-cent financial ledger math; and I went deep into Android systems architecture, background services, and SQLite persistence.*  
>  
> *It taught me extreme discipline, product ownership, and how to write production software with automated tests that doesn't fail under concurrency. Now, I am fully focused on channeling all this technical energy into a full-time engineering role on a high-impact team."*

Notice what this script does:
1. It explains the hustle with dignity and confidence (no apologies).
2. It immediately pivots to the impressive code you built.
3. It highlights qualities employers love: discipline, independence, and deep production knowledge.
