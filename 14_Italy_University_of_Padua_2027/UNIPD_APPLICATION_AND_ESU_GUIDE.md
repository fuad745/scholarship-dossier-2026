## 💰 Funding & Full Scholarship Schemes

Admitted students from Ethiopia have access to two major funding routes:

### 1. Veneto ESU Right-to-Education Full Scholarship (*Borsa di Studio ESU*)
* **100% Tuition Exemption:** University tuition fees waived down to regional tax (€170/year, reimbursed upon award).
* **Free Student Accommodation:** Free private or double room in official university residences managed by ESU Padova.
* **Daily Free Meals:** Free daily meals at university student cafeterias (*mense universitarie*).
* **Annual Cash Grant:** Direct bank deposit of up to **~€7,000 / year** cash.
* **Why Fuad Qualifies:** Under Italian law (D.M. 1320/2021), Ethiopia is officially designated as a developing country (*Paese particolarmente povero e in via di sviluppo*). Applicants from Ethiopia qualify for maximum regional financial bracket (*fascia 1*) via standard consular family income certification.

### 2. Padua International Excellence Scholarship
* **Merit-Based Award:** **€8,000 / year** plus full tuition fee waiver.
* **No Separate Application:** All top-ranking international applicants admitted during Round 1 are automatically considered.
