### Engineering Practice & Systems Problem Solving
Rather than treating software engineering as theoretical exercises, my focus has consistently been on engineering software that operates reliably in constrained, high-stakes environments:

1. **Transactional Integrity & Concurrency Architecture:** In developing *LuckyDraw*, a high-volume Telegram Mini App lottery engine built on Laravel and Livewire, I addressed race conditions and double-spending risks by enforcing atomic row-locking mechanisms (`DB::transaction` with `lockForUpdate`) and strict integer-cent financial ledger arithmetic (`App\Support\Money`). To validate system resilience under high concurrency, I engineered automated test suites covering deadlock avoidance, stuck-draw recovery, and HMAC-authenticated payload verification.
2. **Geospatial Systems & Crowdsourced Consensus:** In creating *Addis Condo Finder*, I built a cross-platform Flutter application and Laravel backend to map complex condominium developments across Addis Ababa. I implemented bounding-box viewport queries, offline-first local caching, and a multi-user verification pipeline requiring independent consensus before crowdsourced geospatial coordinates are published.
3. **Android Systems & Low-Level Lifecycle:** Through direct interaction with mobile operating system internals, I have engineered persistent Android Foreground Services, inter-process communication (IPC) routines, and battery-optimization bypass mechanisms, ensuring high-frequency background synchronization across real-world field devices.
