# China CSC Local Document Clearance & Authentication Guide (Addis Ababa)

This guide outlines how to obtain the two local Ethiopian administrative documents required for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B) and Chinese university portals: the **Certificate of Non-Criminal Record (Police Clearance)** and the **English Medium of Instruction Certificate**.

---

## 1. Certificate of Non-Criminal Record (Police Clearance)
Chinese universities and the CSC require proof that the applicant has no criminal convictions, issued within the last 6 months.

* **Issuing Authority:** Federal Police Commission Forensics Investigation Department (የፌደራል ፖሊስ ወንጀል ምርመራ ቢሮ / ፎረንሲክ)
* **Location in Addis Ababa:** Near Torhailoch / Mexico, Addis Ababa.
* **Requirements to Bring:**
  1. Original Ethiopian Passport (`E00340202`) + 2 photocopies.
  2. Original Kebele Resident ID card / National ID (Fayda) + 2 photocopies.
  3. 2 passport-sized photographs (recent, white background).
  4. Application processing fee (paid via Telebirr or CBE).
* **Processing Steps:**
  1. Visit the office in the morning (arrive by 8:00 AM).
  2. Complete the biometric fingerprint scanning.
  3. The certificate is issued in English on official security paper bearing the Federal Police stamp and watermark.
  4. Standard turnaround: 2 to 4 working days.
* **Scan & Naming:** Once collected, scan in high resolution (300 DPI) as `Fuad_Ahmed_Police_Clearance_Certificate.pdf`.

---

## 2. English Proficiency Exemption (Medium of Instruction Certificate)
Chinese universities (including Harbin Institute of Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology, and USTC) require proof that you can follow graduate courses in English. **You do NOT need IELTS or TOEFL if you provide an English Medium of Instruction Certificate.**

* **Issuing Authority:** Office of the University Registrar, St. Mary’s University.
* **Location:** St. Mary's University Main Campus, Mexico / Saris, Addis Ababa.
* **What to Request:** *"English Medium of Instruction Letter"* (የማስተማሪያ ቋንቋ እንግሊዝኛ መሆኑን የሚያረጋግጥ ደብዳቤ).
* **Key Content Required in the Letter:**
  * Must state that Mr. Fuad Ahmed was enrolled in the Bachelor of Science program in Computer Science from [Start Year] to July 2022.
  * Must explicitly state: *"The medium of instruction, examinations, assignments, and senior capstone projects for all courses throughout the entire degree program was strictly the English language."*
  * Must be printed on official St. Mary's University letterhead with the Registrar's signature and official university seal.
* **Turnaround:** Usually issued same-day or within 24–48 hours at the Registrar's office.

---

## 3. Ministry Authentication (MoFA & Chinese Embassy) – When is it Needed?
* **For Initial Online Applications (Oct 2026 – Feb 2027):**
  * You do **NOT** need Ministry authentication for the online submission. Clear, color scans of your original St. Mary's Degree, Student Copy Transcript, Passport, and Medical Form are completely sufficient.
* **For Final Visa Application (May – July 2027):**
  * Only after you are officially awarded the scholarship will you take your original degree and police clearance to the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA Ethiopia - Main Office near Mexico)** and subsequently the **Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Addis Ababa** for legalization before receiving your X1 student visa.
