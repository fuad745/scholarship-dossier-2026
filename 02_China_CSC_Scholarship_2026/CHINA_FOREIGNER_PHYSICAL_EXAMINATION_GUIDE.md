# China CSC Foreigner Physical Examination Guide (Ethiopia / Addis Ababa)

For all Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) and university admissions, applicants must submit the official **Foreigner Physical Examination Form (外国人体格检查表)**. Applications with improperly completed medical forms are automatically rejected during preliminary administrative review.

---

## 1. Golden Rules to Avoid Medical Form Rejection
1. **6-Month Validity:** The form is strictly valid for **6 months** from the date of issuance. A checkup completed in October/November 2026 remains valid through April/May 2027, perfectly covering the scholarship decision period.
2. **Two Hospital Stamps (Crucial!):**
   * **Stamp 1 (Photo Seal):** Your passport-sized photograph on the top right MUST be stamped with the hospital's official round stamp overlapping the photo and the paper.
   * **Stamp 2 (Final Conclusion):** The official hospital seal must be stamped at the bottom next to the chief examining physician’s signature.
3. **Doctor's Final Verdict:** The physician must explicitly write in the final conclusion box: **"Healthy - Fit to study abroad"** or **"Suggest Eligible"**, followed by the doctor’s handwritten signature and date.
4. **Keep Original Reports:** Keep the physical laboratory printouts (ECG report, Chest X-Ray film/radiology report, and blood test lab results). You will scan them as attachments with the form, and you will need the physical originals when applying for your X1 Student Visa at the Chinese Embassy in Addis Ababa.

---

## 2. Complete Checklist of Required Medical Tests

| Medical Examination Item | Description / Laboratory Requirement | Status / Result Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Physical Check** | Height, Weight, Blood Pressure, Pulse, Eyesight (Visual Acuity) | Normal ranges |
| **Internal Medicine** | Heart auscultation, Lungs, Abdomen, Lymph nodes | Normal |
| **Surgical Examination** | Spine, Extremities, Skin, Thyroid | Normal |
| **Ophthalmology & ENT** | Color vision (check for color blindness), Ears, Nose, Throat | Normal |
| **Chest X-Ray Examination** | Chest Radiography (Check for Tuberculosis / Pulmonary lesions) | Attached Radiology Report stating "Normal Chest / Clear Lungs" |
| **Electrocardiogram (ECG)** | Cardiac rhythm evaluation | Attached ECG strip/report stating "Normal Sinus Rhythm" |
| **Serological Blood Tests** | **HIV 1/2 antibody screen** (Required) | **Negative** |
| | **Syphilis / VDRL / RPR** (Required) | **Negative** |
| | **Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)** | **Negative** |
| | **Hepatitis C (HCV)** | **Negative** |
| | **Liver Function (ALT / AST)** & **Renal Function (Creatinine / BUN)** | Normal |

## 3. Recommended Hospitals & Diagnostic Centers in Addis Ababa
The Chinese Embassy and CSC require the examination to be conducted by a licensed tertiary hospital or accredited diagnostic center. In Addis Ababa, the following institutions are experienced in processing the Foreigner Physical Examination Form:

1. **Korean Medical Center (MCM - Myungsung Christian Medical Center)**
   * **Location:** Gerji / Bole Sub-City, Addis Ababa
   * **Why MCM:** Highly experienced with foreign embassy health clearances (regularly conducts US, Canadian, and Chinese student health checks). Diagnostic lab results and radiology reports are in standard English format.
2. **St. Paul’s Hospital Millennium Medical College (SPHMMC)**
   * **Location:** Swaziland St, Addis Ababa
   * **Why St. Paul’s:** Major federal referral university hospital. Officially recognized government stamp.
3. **Bethzatha Health Service & Diagnostic Center**
   * **Location:** Stadium area / Bole Road, Addis Ababa
   * **Why Bethzatha:** Fast turnaround for serology, ECG, and digital chest radiography.
4. **International Clinical Laboratories (ICL Ethiopia)**
   * **Location:** Multiple branches across Addis Ababa (Bole, Kera, CMC)
   * **Why ICL:** Internationally accredited laboratory (ISO 15189) for all blood chemistry, HIV, and Hepatitis panels.

## 4. Step-by-Step Clinic Visit Procedure
1. **Print the Form:** Print 2 copies of the official standard two-page *Foreigner Physical Examination Form* in high quality on clean A4 paper.
2. **Affix Photos:** Glue 2 recent passport-size photos with white background to the top right of each form.
3. **Complete the Tests:** Fast for 8–10 hours before the morning visit (required for liver enzymes and blood glucose).
4. **Get the Stamps:** Before leaving the hospital:
   * Verify the doctor has signed the form.
   * Verify the hospital seal is stamped **OVER the corner of your photo**.
   * Verify the hospital seal is stamped **at the bottom of page 2**.
   * Collect all printed test slips: ECG printout, X-Ray radiology report, and blood test lab sheet.
5. **Scan to PDF:** Scan Page 1, Page 2, and the attached lab reports into a single, high-resolution PDF file named `Fuad_Ahmed_Foreigner_Physical_Examination_Report.pdf` (keep under 3MB for portal upload).
