# China CSC Master's Supervisor Outreach & Pre-Acceptance Playbook

This playbook provides the exact, proven strategy, email pitch templates, supervisor directories, and official Pre-Acceptance Letter (PAL) forms to secure a professor’s endorsement at top Chinese universities for the 2026/2027 Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B) and University Fellowships.

---

## 1. Why Professor Outreach is the #1 Key to Winning CSC Type B
* **The "One-University" Rule:** In the official CSC portal (`studyinchina.csc.edu.cn`), you are only allowed to submit **one Type B (University Program)** application per year.
* **The Power of Pre-Acceptance:** When a Chinese professor accepts you into their laboratory and signs a **Provisional Acceptance Letter (PAL)**, they submit your dossier directly to the university's Graduate School / International College. At institutions like HIT, BIT, and USTC, applicants with a signed supervisor acceptance letter have an estimated **>90% scholarship award rate**.
* **Prime Window:** **October to November**. Professors receive dozens of inquiries in December and January once quotas are already filled. Reaching out right now gives you first-mover advantage.
